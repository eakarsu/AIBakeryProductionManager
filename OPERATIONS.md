# Governed production planning

The governed planner deterministically converts versioned demand, recipes, inventory, equipment, and capacity into batch requirements. Inventory shortages, capacity, maintenance, and quality holds block execution. It never substitutes ingredients automatically. QA and production approvals are separate, HACCP critical controls and allergen-label verification gate release, and lot provenance supports recall tracing.

Bootstrap/migrations/guarded seed are explicit; start is non-mutating. AI action and generated gap routes are unmounted. POS/orders, procurement/inventory, equipment telemetry, food-safety logs, delivery, yield/shelf-life validation, camera grading, and HACCP/regulatory sign-off require approved integrations and qualified operators before production use.
